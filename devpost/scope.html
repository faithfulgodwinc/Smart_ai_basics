Approved

Subscription & Trial Tracker

A calm, secure-feeling place to log your free trials and subscriptions, see which card each one is charging, and get reminded before you're billed, with a clear path to cancel. (Name to be decided.)

The unique kernel

It tracks which card is exposed, not only when you'll be charged. Each subscription carries its own cancel route, so acting on a reminder takes one tap.

Main account

The one you protect. You should see right away what's sitting on it.

Side account

The one you top up and pay online from.

The core loop

Highlighted steps are where the kernel shows up: the card grouping, reminders that point straight to the cancel route, and the renewal check that keeps watching after a deadline passes.

Who it's for

Young people who sign up for free trials and then forget: students, friends and family, early-career people, and young business owners trying out apps who are “too busy, and then they forget.” Many, like you, keep a protected main account and pay online from a side account.

Today they find out from a bank alert, sometimes months late.

What “working” looks like

In a one-minute demo: add a few subscriptions across two cards, see them grouped with monthly totals per card and overall, then a real reminder email arrives (and an in-app reminder shows) with clear cancel directions, like “Google Pay → Payments & subscriptions → Cancel.” Mark one cancelled, and the totals go down.

Nobody can wait days on camera, so a reminder can be fired on demand for the demo.

“Something very presentable and something that works totally.”

Inspiration & identity

  • Opening it feels like entering “a whole world of comfort, security, and calmness.”
  • Apple design principles: white space, neat, friendly, calm.
  • Samsung Health's soft feel, with a restrained palette instead of “too many colors.”
  • Never “very coded or heavy,” never AI-generated or generic.

Why it matters to you

Surprise charges landed on the main account you protect, and Netflix billed you for months unnoticed. You also want to own the code: “what powers what,” especially how sending emails works.

The boundary

Now proof of concept
  • Manual entry: name, cost, trial end date, card, cancel link and/or your own steps
  • Grouped by card, with projected monthly totals per card and overall
  • Real reminder emails before the deadline, plus an in-app reminder
  • Mark as cancelled, which updates the totals
  • Deadline passed unmarked → “needs check”: “Did you cancel, or did it renew?” Renewed → you enter the next billing date, then it's active again with reminders continuing
  • Fire a reminder on demand for the demo
Later worth doing
  • Bank statement upload to detect subscriptions automatically (reading messy statements accurately is a project of its own)
  • Built-in cancel guides for common services (Netflix, Claude, Google Pay…)
  • Market-ready polish for wider use
Cut with reason
  • Truly automatic cancelling or card switching. It would need access to each company's systems, which most don't offer. Here, “click to cancel” opens the right page or shows the right steps.
