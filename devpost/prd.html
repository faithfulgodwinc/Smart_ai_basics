Smart

Be way ahead.

A calm, black-and-white app where young people log their trials and subscriptions under the card each one charges, see their monthly total, and get a reminder email before they're billed, with the exact place to go to cancel.

What became concreteScope said "get reminded before you're billed." Now we know the reminder goes out 2 days and 1 day before, it's sent immediately if one is already due, the email gives a link or an app name depending on where they subscribed, the "Open Smart" button leads to a "Have you cancelled this?" check, and the reminders stop once they answer.

Now vs. later

Building now

  • Landing page
  • Home: card carousel, rows, totals, floating nav, +
  • Add/edit cards (no number) & subscriptions
  • Email captured in the add flow; Profile
  • Real reminder emails (2 days + 1 day; immediately if due)
  • "Have you cancelled this?" loop
  • Free trials flagged, labelled, listed first
  • Cancelled history
  • Details view; data persists

Deferred / later

  • Continue with Google, multi-device
  • Update buttons inside the email
  • Cartoon greeter
  • Yearly billing cycles (cost is monthly)
  • Bank statement upload
  • Built-in cancel guides per service
  • Choosing reminder timing

Never (non-goals)

  • Automatic cancelling or card switching
  • Card numbers or bank connections
  • Salary/budget comparison
  • Phone push notifications

Confirmed

  • Totals are projected. A free trial's monthly cost counts while it's active (what it would charge), and drops out once cancelled.
